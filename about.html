OUR STORY

It All Starts With a Pop

La Pops is a proudly growing popcorn brand built around one simple idea: popcorn doesn't have to be ordinary.

We create exciting popcorn flavours that bring together familiar favourites, creative combinations and a touch of South African personality.

From classic caramel and buttery popcorn to bold savoury flavours and gourmet-inspired creations, La Pops is about giving customers something delicious, memorable and fun.

Our goal is to create popcorn that can be enjoyed every day while also looking right at home at celebrations, parties, corporate events, gifting occasions and sophisticated snack tables.

OUR MISSION

Making Every Pop Count

We want La Pops to become a popcorn brand known for creativity, quality, presentation and unforgettable flavour.

As the business grows, our vision is to develop a wider collection of products and experiences while keeping the personality that makes La Pops unique.
